# Email intake (marketing replies → Chatwoot → CRM)

> Status: **Live** (2026-09-30). Proven end-to-end with real mail: a Gmail reply
> to an ARTIMA drip email became a Chatwoot conversation, a CRM activity on the
> existing person, and a deal attach with last-touch attribution. A reply sent
> from Chatwoot reached Gmail in-thread, passing DKIM, SPF and DMARC. No real
> lead has answered a journey email yet, because every drip is paused.

The email analog of [social-intake](./social-intake.md). Marketing email goes out
from Dittofeed (see [`docs/marketing-engine-dittofeed.md`](../../../docs/marketing-engine-dittofeed.md)) under a per-brand persona. When a lead
replies, the reply lands in that brand's Chatwoot inbox. The CRM then logs it
like a DM: one `inboundActivity` per conversation, and the
[lead pipeline](../systems/lead-pipeline.md) attaches or opens the deal.
Managers answer from the CRM's **Conversations** tab, and the answer goes out
as the brand persona.

```
Dittofeed ──Resend──▶ lead's inbox
                          │ reply
                          ▼
Resend receiving (MX on mail.<brand>) ──email.received──▶ n8n "Resend Receive → Chatwoot"
   fetch full email + raw MIME · drop auto-replies · stamp X-Original-To
                          │ POST message/rfc822
                          ▼
Chatwoot ActionMailbox relay ──▶ brand email inbox (conversation)
                          │ conversation_created webhook
                          ▼
n8n "Email Intake → CRM" ── person by email · Dittofeed attribution ──▶ inboundActivity(EMAIL_MESSAGE)
                          │
                          ▼
lead pipeline: attach to open deal / open a new one (source EMAIL), sequencing, marketing sync
```

## 1. Receive and relay (n8n `Wo0c6KBIYbTkykWN`)

A Resend webhook (event `email.received` only) calls
`/webhook/resend-inbound-<secret>`. The event carries metadata only, so the flow
does the rest:

1. **Accept.** Only `email.received` events are handled. The last 500
   `email_id`s are remembered, so a Resend retry can't open a second
   conversation.
2. **Fetch.** `GET api.resend.com/emails/receiving/{id}`, using the
   *Resend Receiving API* credential (a Full-access key; sending-only keys can't
   read received mail). The flow then downloads `raw.download_url`, a signed link
   valid for about an hour that needs no auth.
3. **Classify.** Judged on the **raw headers**. The email is **dropped** when:
   - `Auto-Submitted` is anything but `no`;
   - it carries `X-Autoreply`, `X-Autorespond`, `X-Autogenerated` or `X-Failed-Recipients`;
   - `Precedence` is bulk, junk, list or auto_reply;
   - it has `List-Id` or `List-Unsubscribe`;
   - it's a `multipart/report`, or `Return-Path` is `<>`;
   - the sender is mailer-daemon, postmaster or noreply;
   - the subject is an out-of-office phrase, in EN, RO, RU, DE or FR;
   - it was sent from one of our own inboxes (a loop);
   - it isn't addressed to one of the four personas.
4. **Relay.** The flow prepends `X-Original-To: <persona>` (so Bcc'd and
   forwarded copies still route) and POSTs the MIME to
   `chat.enso.ro/rails/action_mailbox/relay/inbound_emails`. It authenticates with
   basic auth, user `actionmailbox`, using the *Chatwoot Email Relay* credential.

Signature verification is skipped on purpose. The flow re-fetches every email
from Resend by id, so a forged event can only point at a real email in our own
account.

## 2. Chatwoot email inboxes

- **Setup.** Inboxes #11–#14 have IMAP off and SMTP on, and send through Resend
  (`smtp.resend.com:465`, the *Chatwoot SMTP* key). The inbox's `email` and
  `forward_to_email` are both the persona address; `EmailChannelFinder` matches
  To, Cc, X-Original-To and Bcc against them. Anything unmatched falls to
  `DefaultMailbox` and is dropped.
- **Sender name.** `reply_mailer_migration` is **on** for the account. With it
  on, From = Reply-To = the persona. With it off, Chatwoot writes
  `reply+<uuid>@false`. The sender name type is *professional*
  (`business_name <email>`).
- **Relay auth.** `RAILS_INBOUND_EMAIL_SERVICE=relay` and
  `RAILS_INBOUND_EMAIL_PASSWORD` are set on chatwoot-web.
- **Storage.** ActiveStorage is on the Railway bucket `enso-chatwoot-files`
  (`s3_compatible`). The old local disk was wiped on every deploy. That broke
  inbound routing (`FileNotFoundError`) and lost about 430 attachment blobs from
  before 2026-09-30.
- **The UI hides the SMTP form** unless IMAP is on. The mailer only checks
  `smtp_enabled`, so the key was set from a Rails runner.

## 3. Email Intake → CRM (n8n `hGMinRRS8YSPGZhp`)

A second account webhook in Chatwoot (`conversation_created`) calls
`/webhook/chatwoot-email-intake-<secret>`.
[Social intake](./social-intake.md) skips email inboxes and this flow skips
social ones, so neither handles the other's conversations.

1. **Resolve.** The flow only acts on inboxes 11–14 with an incoming message,
   and skips anything Chatwoot flags as an `auto_reply`. It takes the sender's
   address and name, the subject, and `processed_message_content` (the reply
   without the quoted original). The inbox gives the project.
   - It marks the activity **synthetic** when the name or text matches
     `test|tst|qa|demo|example`, the same rule social intake uses. Synthetic
     activities never open or attach a deal.
2. **Deduplicate.** Skips when an activity already exists for that
   `chatwootConversationId`.
3. **Person.** Match on `emails.primaryEmail` (case-insensitive exact
   match, with wildcards escaped), oldest first. If there's no match, create the person.
4. **Attribution.** See the next section.
5. **Create the activity.** `createInboundActivity` with:
   - `kind EMAIL_MESSAGE`, `source CHATWOOT`, `trafficType EMAIL`
   - `platformUserId` = the sender address
   - `sourceExternalId` = the incoming Message-ID
   - `chatwootConversationId` and `externalThreadId` = the conversation id
   - `body` = `Subject: …`, then the "Replying to …" line, then the reply text

CRM side, from PR #292:
- `EMAIL_MESSAGE` maps to deal source `EMAIL` and the label "Email".
- The timeline reads "Captured from an email", but only on a person's first touch.
- Marketing sync sends `inbound_message`, the same drip-exit signal a DM sends.
- An email reply after enrollment **connects the deal** in the
  sequencing engine, recording `firstContactChannel EMAIL`.
- **No consent is granted.** A reply opens a reply window only (see
  [consent](../systems/consent.md)).
- The metadata options `inboundActivity.kind = EMAIL_MESSAGE`,
  `opportunity.source = EMAIL` and `person.leadSource = EMAIL` were added in
  Settings → Data model.

## 4. Attribution: which email did they answer?

Dittofeed records every journey email it sends against the lead. Its `userId` is
the **CRM person id**, and each record holds the journey, template, rendered
subject and send time.

The flow reads them with the *Dittofeed Admin API* credential:
- `GET marketing.enso.ro/api/admin/deliveries?workspaceId=…&channels=Email&userId=<personId>`, falling back to `&to=<sender>`
- `/api/admin/journeys` and `/api/admin/content/templates` for the names

**How it picks.** It considers deliveries sent before the reply:
1. The first choice is the one whose subject matches the reply's subject, after
   stripping `Re:`, `Fwd:`, `Ответ:`, `Răspuns:` and similar.
2. Otherwise it takes the latest one from the last 30 days.
3. Otherwise there's no attribution, and the email counts as a direct write-in.

The lookups can't block intake: they are set to continue on error.

| Field on the activity | Value |
|---|---|
| UTM Source / Medium | `dittofeed` / `email` |
| UTM Campaign | journey name, e.g. `ARTIMA Introductory` |
| UTM Content | template name (current name, so renames apply retroactively) |
| Body, line 2 | `Replying to: "<subject exactly as sent>" (sent 13 Jun 2026, journey …)`, frozen at send time |

The stock attribution code carries these to the deal: the `last*` UTMs and Last
Traffic Type for an existing deal, the first-touch UTMs for a new one, and the
Google Chat post. A journey name never re-homes a deal, because only campaign
slugs registered on `Project.utmCampaigns` do that.

Template editor **test sends are not deliveries** and are never attributed.

## 5. Replying from the CRM

The deal's and person's **Conversations** tab lists email threads next to DMs.
A manager's reply goes out through Chatwoot as their own agent: the CRM creates
the agent on first use and adds it to every inbox. The lead sees the persona
(`Oleg de la ARTIMA <oleg@mail.artima.md>`), in the same thread.

Each panel reply is logged (PR #295):
- **An outbound activity.** `channel` comes from the inbox (EMAIL, SOCIAL,
  WHATSAPP or SMS), `loggedVia` is CRM_INITIATED, `performedBy` is the manager,
  the email subject is saved as `Re: …`, and `toIdentity` is the contact's
  address.
- **A timeline sentence**, "by <manager>": "Replied by email · Re: …" or
  "Replied on Instagram".

## 6. Dittofeed delivery events

Resend webhooks are **account-wide**, and Dittofeed's endpoint rejects any event
without Dittofeed tags with a 400. Pointed at Dittofeed directly, CRM and
Chatwoot mail would get the webhook auto-disabled, which is how it had been for
months. So Resend's delivery webhook (sent, delivered, delayed, bounced,
complained, opened, clicked) goes through a router instead: n8n
`lT5JpY4eCvRSjmlo` at `/webhook/resend-events-<secret>`.

The router:
- forwards only events carrying `tags.workspaceId` to
  `marketing.enso.ro/api/public/webhooks/resend`, as the untouched raw body plus
  the svix headers, so the signature still verifies;
- returns Dittofeed's own status to Resend, so retries still work;
- answers 200 to everything else.

## Known limits

- **One CRM activity per Chatwoot conversation**, as with social. A follow-up
  email in an existing thread shows in Chatwoot but isn't logged again, even
  after the conversation auto-resolved. A fresh email (new subject or thread)
  is logged.
- **Replies typed in Chatwoot's own UI** are delivered but not logged as
  outbound activity. Only the CRM panel logs.
- **Template names are not snapshotted** by Dittofeed, so UTM Content shows
  today's name. The "Replying to" line is the faithful record.
- **Stop-on-reply for drips is not configured.** The `inbound_message` signal is
  there, but no journey has an exit on it yet.

## Operations

- **n8n credentials:**
  - *Resend Receiving API* (locked to `api.resend.com`)
  - *Chatwoot Email Relay* (locked to `chat.enso.ro`)
  - *Dittofeed Admin API* (locked to `marketing.enso.ro`)
  - *Twenty CRM API*

  Secrets were entered by a human through a hidden-input Terminal script and
  never displayed. Run such scripts in macOS Terminal: the Claude app's terminal
  pane echoes hidden input.
- **Webhook paths** carry a random secret suffix. Read them from the n8n workflow
  if needed.
- **Replay a dropped reply.** Replay the Resend event from the Resend webhook
  page. The dedupe only remembers ids that were accepted.
- **Test safely.**
  - Put "test" in the name or text for a synthetic run: it creates the person
    and activity but no deal.
  - Soft-delete the test activity and its `timelineActivity` rows afterwards.
  - Remember that a real reply attaches to the sender's open deal and can ping
    its owner.
